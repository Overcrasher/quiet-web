Welcome to Quiet Web

Says no to cookie banners and closes newsletter popups, so you don't have to.

Try it now

  1. 1Visit a news site: the cookie banner is answered "no" for you.
  2. 2Click the Quiet Web icon to see what was handled on that page.
  3. 3If a site looks broken, switch Quiet Web off for that site from the popup.

Good to know

  • Quiet Web only ever clicks reject or "necessary only" buttons, never accept.
  • Everything happens on your device. No account, no analytics, no servers, no network requests.

Pin it: click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin.
